Version 3.7.1 brings the per-meal breakdown back to Home on phones, fixes weights of 100 kg and over, and lets self-hosted instances close sign-ups.

## What changed

- Home on a phone shows calories per meal again, under the macro rows, as it does on desktop
- Weight takes four digits in kg, so 100.5 kg no longer loses its last digit
- The Docs link in the header opens the documentation instead of a missing page
- Self-hosting: ENABLE_SIGNUPS=0 closes registration after the first account. No new database migrations

As always, everything here is in the open-source repository, and your data stays exportable.
