A disclosure first: I build MacroTrackr, which is one of the apps on this list. I have tried to be fair to the others, and for plenty of people one of them is the better choice. Each section says who it suits.

Most people looking for a MyFitnessPal alternative have hit one of three things: the barcode scanner, which moved to Premium on 1 October 2022 ([MyFitnessPal](https://blog.myfitnesspal.com/barcode-scan-moving-to-myfitnesspal-premium/)); the ads on the free plan; or a database full of duplicate user-submitted entries. Which of those bothers you most decides which app below fits.

Prices differ by country and change often, so I have left most of them out. Check the app store listing for yours.

## Cronometer: if you care about micronutrients

Cronometer tracks vitamins and minerals in detail, not just calories and macros, and its food entries lean on curated nutrition databases rather than whatever users typed in. If you want to know whether you are short on iron or vitamin D, it is the strongest option here.

- **Free plan:** barcode scanning and custom macro targets, with ads.
- **Paid plan (Gold):** removes ads and adds more analysis.
- **Trade-off:** the screens are dense. If you only care about protein, carbs and fat, there is a lot to look past.

## MacroFactor: if you want the app to set your targets

MacroFactor estimates your energy expenditure from what you log and how your weight trends, then adjusts your calorie and macro targets each week. Plenty of people like handing that decision over.

- **Pricing:** subscription only, after a 7-day trial. At the time of writing the US App Store lists $71.99 a year.
- **Trade-off:** there is no free plan, and the targets are coached for you. If you prefer to set your own, you are paying for a feature you will not use.

## Lose It!: if you mainly count calories

Lose It! is built around a simple daily calorie budget and is quick to log in. The barcode scanner is free.

- **Free plan:** calorie tracking and barcode scanning, with ads.
- **Paid plan (Premium):** adds custom macro goals and more detailed reports.
- **Trade-off:** if your targets are in grams of protein rather than total calories, the parts you need are behind Premium.

## FatSecret: if you want the most for free

FatSecret keeps calorie and macro tracking and the barcode scanner on its free plan.

- **Free plan:** food diary, barcode scanning and macros, with ads.
- **Paid plan (Premium):** removes ads and adds extra features.
- **Trade-off:** it looks and feels older than the others, and the ads on the free plan are noticeable.

## MacroTrackr: if you want macro targets without ads, or to host it yourself

This is the one I build. It is aimed at people who set gram targets for protein, carbs and fat and want to see their weekly average rather than chase a perfect day.

- **Free plan:** logging with no daily cap, barcode scanning, custom macro targets, the last 7 days of progress and 5 saved meals. No ads on any plan.
- **Paid plan (Pro):** your full history, 30 and 90 day views, unlimited saved meals and CSV export. See [pricing](/pricing).
- **Self-hosting:** the code is open source under AGPLv3, so you can [run it on your own server](/open-source) for free with every feature.
- **Trade-off:** it is newer and smaller than everything else on this list. The food database is [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/) plus your own foods, so a regional product may be missing until someone adds it. It installs as an app from the browser rather than from the iOS App Store.

## Which one to pick

- You want micronutrients: **Cronometer**.
- You want the app to adjust your targets for you, and do not mind paying: **MacroFactor**.
- You count calories and want it simple: **Lose It!**
- You want the most features without paying: **FatSecret**.
- You set macro targets yourself, want no ads, or want to own your data: **MacroTrackr**.

## Taking your MyFitnessPal history with you

Whichever you choose, export your MyFitnessPal diary first. MyFitnessPal currently limits file export to Premium and Premium+ accounts ([MyFitnessPal](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032273352-Data-Export-FAQs)), so if you are on Premium, do it before you cancel. The [MyFitnessPal export guide](/migrate/myfitnesspal) walks through the steps, and MacroTrackr can import the file directly. For a side-by-side on features, see [MacroTrackr vs MyFitnessPal](/compare/myfitnesspal).
